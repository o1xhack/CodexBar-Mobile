CodexBar is an iPhone/iPad companion for the Mac app. No separate account login is required. For review without a Mac, dismiss the initial Release Notes with Done, then tap View Demo on the Usage tab while it shows Waiting for Mac Sync to explore synthetic provider usage and costs. Live data requires the Mac companion on the same iCloud account. Mac download: https://github.com/o1xhack/CodexBar-Mobile/releases .
