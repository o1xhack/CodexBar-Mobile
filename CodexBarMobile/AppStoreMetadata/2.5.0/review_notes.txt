CodexBar is an iPhone/iPad companion for the Mac app. No separate account login is required. For review without a Mac, dismiss the initial Release Notes with Done, then tap View Demo on the Usage tab while it shows Waiting for Mac Sync to explore synthetic provider usage and costs. Live data requires the Mac companion on the same iCloud account. Mac download: https://github.com/o1xhack/CodexBar-Mobile/releases . Version 2.5 adds per-account usage cards, pinning and ordering of usage cards (stored only on the device), quota pace for more providers calculated on the iPhone, a new Quota pace widget, and Contact / More Apps links in Settings (Contact opens the mail app to codexbar@yuxiaow.com; More Apps lists the developer's other apps and projects). This release is iOS-only; the paired Mac version remains 0.70.0.1 Mobile 2.4.0.
